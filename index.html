The problem

Your tools each hold part of the record. People hold it together.

Every project runs a CDE, a project system, email and chat. None of them owns the handoff between them — so a person does, until they don't.

  1. 01 · Approvals

    Approvals stall in inboxes

    A submittal waits on a reviewer who's on site. Nothing prompts them until the programme slips.

  2. 02 · Drawings

    Superseded drawings stay in use

    The new revision is issued. The old PDF is still in an inbox — and on the bench on site.

  3. 03 · Defects

    Defects sit open

    Snags get logged. Closing them depends on someone remembering to chase.

  4. 04 · Evidence

    The evidence lives in email

    Who was told, and when, is scattered across threads — rebuilt by hand when someone asks.

Questions

Before you ask.

Can't see yours? Email info@plunq.co.

Does this replace our CDE or project system?

No. Plunq works alongside the tools your projects already run — Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud, Outlook and Slack today — and handles the handoffs between them. Your CDE stays the source of truth.

Will Plunq change things on our projects without asking?

No. Plunq learns how your projects run and suggests workflows. Nothing runs until someone on your team signs it off — and from then on it follows the same rule every time and records every step.

How is this different from the automations built into our CDE or project system?

Those automate what happens inside one platform. Plunq works across them, so a handoff can start in your project system and finish in someone's inbox or on Slack. And it learns your conventions, instead of handing you a blank rule builder.

We already sync our project system with accounting. Is this the same thing?

No. Those tools keep field and finance data in step. Plunq works on the delivery side — approvals, drawings, defects and the evidence trail your information manager answers for.

Does my team need technical skills?

No. Plunq is built for information managers, document controllers and quality leads. You review each suggested workflow on a visual canvas, adjust it with point-and-click, and test it before it goes live.

What happens if something fails?

Plunq retries automatically, so a slow system or a brief outage doesn't lose anything. If a step still fails, the record shows exactly where and why, and you can retry the run from the dashboard.

How do you look after our logins?

OAuth tokens, API keys and secrets are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, kept separate for each organisation, and only decrypted in memory when a step runs. Disconnect a tool and its stored credentials are deleted straight away.

We use a tool that isn't listed. Can you add it?

Probably. Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud, Outlook and Slack work today. Asite, SharePoint and Teams are next, with Aconex, Dalux and PlanRadar on the roadmap. Tell us what you run when you request access.

What does it cost?

Pricing is set to your projects. We are working with a small group of early-access teams on preferential terms while they help shape the product.
