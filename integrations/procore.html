Procore

Together

What Plunq and Procore do together

  • 01 · Watches

    Starts on the event

    An RFI, submittal, punch item, observation, daily log or change order sets the workflow going.

  • 02 · Chases

    Chases what's late

    Plunq watches review windows and due dates, sends the chase, and escalates when it slips.

  • 03 · Writes back

    Keeps the record in Procore

    Raises RFIs and submittals, replies, updates status, logs punch items and change orders.

  • 04 · Routes

    Sends it to the right people

    Reads the company and project on each record, so nothing crosses between jobs.

  • 05 · Connects

    Reaches your other tools

    A handoff can start in Procore and finish in Autodesk Construction Cloud, Outlook or Slack.

  • 06 · Proves

    Leaves the evidence

    Every step is logged, a slow call is retried four times, and failures show where they stopped.
